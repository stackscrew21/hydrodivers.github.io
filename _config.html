Tether Cable: The Lifeline to Control
The tether cable is a critical component of our ROV system, providing a reliable communication and power link between the operator and the underwater vehicle. While radio frequency waves struggle to penetrate water, our tether ensures seamless live video transmission and control.
In order to perform remote inspection tasks, live video is needed at the surface so that decisions by humans can be made on navigating the vehicle and inspecting the target.

The ROV System: Integrated Control and Monitoring
Our ROV system integrates seamlessly, providing intuitive control and comprehensive monitoring capabilities. The operator uses a joystick to send input to a Python program, which then relays commands to a microcontroller that manages the motors, pneumatic system, and cameras.
Operator Control
Joystick interface for intuitive maneuvering.
Microcontroller

Custom PCB

Motor Drivers
High-current control for robust performance.
